# LabelIQ

**AI-Powered Legal Metrology Compliance Platform**

LabelIQ helps consumers, inspectors, and manufacturers analyze packaged commodity labels and identify potential Legal Metrology compliance issues.

## 🚀 Live Demo

👉 [Open LabelIQ](https://labeliq-sepia.vercel.app/)

## 🎯 Problem

Manually checking packaged commodity labels for Legal Metrology requirements can be time-consuming and error-prone. Important declarations such as MRP, net quantity, manufacturer details, country of origin, and consumer care information need to be verified.

## 💡 Solution

LabelIQ uses OCR, computer vision, rule-based validation, and AI-assisted reasoning to analyze product labels and identify potential compliance issues.

## ✨ Key Features

- Product label scanning
- OCR-based text extraction
- Computer vision and layout analysis
- Legal Metrology rule validation
- MRP verification
- Net quantity verification
- Manufacturer information verification
- Country of origin verification
- Consumer care information verification
- Package vs e-commerce comparison
- Compliance score and risk classification
- Violation and warning detection
- Evidence-based explanations
- Compliance reports
- Digital Compliance Passport
- Manufacturer pre-publish checking

## 👥 User Roles

### Consumer
Scan a product package and understand important compliance information.

### Inspector
Investigate potential violations, review evidence, and generate compliance reports.

### Manufacturer
Upload packaging artwork before printing to identify and correct potential compliance issues.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Upload product images, PDFs, artwork, or e-commerce information.
2. Extract information using OCR and computer vision.
3. Create structured product data.
4. Apply applicable Legal Metrology rules.
5. Compare information across sources.
6. Identify violations, warnings, and missing declarations.
7. Generate explanations and corrective suggestions.
8. Produce compliance results and verification records.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- Frontend: React.js, Vite, Tailwind CSS
- Backend: Python 3.13, FastAPI
- Intelligence & Processing: OpenCV, BeautifulSoup, Requests
- Database & Storage: SQLite, SQLAlchemy, Local File Storage
